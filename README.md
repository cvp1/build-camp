# Build Camp

### ▶ New here? Start at **[cvp1.github.io/build-camp](https://cvp1.github.io/build-camp/)**. Play first, read later.

**You can build software now by describing it,** on the laptop or phone you already
have. This camp is the practice: small things you actually want, then harder ones.

---

## Try it in 5 minutes. No install; a free Claude login is enough.

Go to **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**, sign in, and paste one of these:

> Ask me 3 quick questions about what I'm into, one at a time. Then build me a small
> game or tool based on my answers that works on my phone.

> Make me a game that works on my phone: I'm a truck dodging cows on a desert highway.
> Hold left or right to steer, it speeds up over time, show my high score. When it's
> done, ask me what I want to change.

> Make a reaction-time test that works on my phone so I can challenge my friends. Big
> button, five rounds, average time at the end, and a rank from sloth to fighter pilot.

It builds it right there and you can play it. Then tell it what to change: *"make
it harder"*, *"add a second player"*, *"make it look like an 80s arcade"*. That's the
whole trick. **You describe it; it builds it; you steer.**

Now make up your own. That's Mission 0.

---

## Where this goes

| After | You'll have built |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | A game or tool, just by describing it |
| Week 1 | Your own page, live on the internet if you choose |
| Month 1 | A Discord bot, app or tracker that you and your friends actually use |
| Month 2 | Something that runs by itself every day and tells you when it breaks |
| After that | Tools for real systems: homes, farms, shops |

Along the way you learn the part most people skip: how to catch the AI when it's wrong,
how to plan something big, and how to make it remember how you like things done.

## Ready for the real tools? (Mission 1 onward)

**What this costs.** This kit and Mission 0 are free. Mission 1 onward uses Claude Code,
which needs a paid Claude plan. The cheapest is Pro: $20 a month, or $17 a month if
you pay for a year. You'll also need a laptop and about half an hour for setup and
your first build.

1. **Install Claude Code:** https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview
   Follow the step for your computer.
2. **Download the kit:**
   https://github.com/cvp1/build-camp/archive/refs/heads/master.zip
   Unzip it. You'll get a folder called `build-camp-master`.
3. **Open a terminal in that folder:**
   - **Mac:** right-click the folder → New Terminal at Folder.
   - **Windows 11:** right-click the folder → Open in Terminal.
   - **Windows 10:** open the folder, hold Shift, right-click an empty spot → Open
     PowerShell window here.
   - **Chromebook:** in Settings, search for "Linux" and turn on the Linux development
     environment. Use the Terminal app it adds, and the Linux install steps.
4. **Type `claude`, press Enter, then say:** *Let's start.*

Claude asks what you're into, builds your first thing with you on the spot, and plans
the rest around you.

**A school or work laptop may block the install.** If it does, you haven't lost
anything: keep building in claude.ai, where every Mission 0 idea still works.

Already use GitHub? You can click **Use this template** instead of downloading the zip.
You choose whether your copy is public or private.
