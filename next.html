STEP 3

Say hello

Open a terminal in the kit folder, type claude and press Enter. Then say:

Read PROGRESS.md and tell me where we are.

Mac: right-click the folder → New Terminal at Folder.
Windows 11: right-click the folder → Open in Terminal.

It asks what you're into, builds your first thing with you on the spot, and plans the next levels around you.
