What makes it work

  1. Post it as a Reel.It's a vertical video, and Reels are how Instagram shows video.
  2. Words on screen in the first second.The Instagram cut opens with "Watch a sentence become a game."
  3. All text in the middle band.Instagram crops the top and bottom in the feed and on your grid. Our normal cut loses its captions there; this cut doesn't.
  4. The caption's first line is the hook.Only that line shows before "more".
  5. Put the link in your bio.Links in captions can't be tapped, so the end card says "Link in bio".
  6. Add music in Instagram when you post.Their library is licensed, and I can't hear to choose a track.
