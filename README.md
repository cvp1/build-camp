# Build Camp

### ▶ New here? Start at **[cvp1.github.io/build-camp](https://cvp1.github.io/build-camp/)**. Play first, read later.

**You can build software now by describing it.** Not someday: today, on the laptop you
already have. The people who learn to direct AI well are building in a weekend what
used to take a team a month.

This camp teaches you to be one of them, by building things you actually want.

---

## Try it in 5 minutes. No install, no account setup beyond a free login.

Go to **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**, sign in, and paste one of these:

> Make me a playable browser game: I'm a truck dodging cows on a desert highway.
> Arrow keys to steer, it speeds up over time, high score on screen.

> Build me a tool where I paste a used car listing (price, year, miles) and it
> tells me if it's a good deal, with a color-coded verdict and the reasoning.

> Make a reaction-time test I can challenge my friends with. Big button, five
> rounds, average time at the end, a rank from "sloth" to "fighter pilot".

It builds it right there and you can play it. Then tell it what to change: *"make
it harder"*, *"add a boost button"*, *"make it look like a 1980s arcade"*. That's the
whole trick. **You describe it; it builds it; you steer.**

Now make up your own. That's Mission 0.

---

## Where this goes

| After | You'll have built |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | A game or tool, just by describing it |
| Week 1 | Your own website, live on the internet |
| Month 1 | A Discord bot, app or tracker that you and your friends actually use |
| Month 2 | Something that runs by itself every day and tells you when it breaks |
| After that | Tools for real systems: homes, farms, shops |

Along the way you learn the part most people skip: how to catch the AI when it's wrong,
how to plan something big, and how to make it remember how you like things done.

## Ready for the real tools? (Mission 1+)
1. Install Claude Code: https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code/overview
2. Get your own copy of this kit: click **Use this template** at the top of this page.
3. Open a terminal in your copy, run `claude`, and type:
   *"Read PROGRESS.md and tell me where we are."*

Claude asks you a few questions about what you're into, builds you something
small on the spot, and plans the rest around you.
